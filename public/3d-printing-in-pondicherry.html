Local service

3D Printing in Pondicherry

I'm Aswin, and I run AswinPrints from Villianur in Pondicherry. If you have been looking for somewhere local to get something 3D printed — a figurine, a replacement part, a model for a college project, a batch of gifts — this page covers what I can make, what it costs, and how to order.

What I print

Everything is printed on a Bambu Lab A1 in PLA. There are currently 59 pieces in the shop, from ₹99 to ₹12,000, across four kinds of thing:

Figurines

Dragons, birds, articulated models and characters — the largest part of the catalogue.

Home decor

Vases, temple models, wall pieces, lamps and lanterns.

Functional parts

Shelves, stands and holders — things that do a job rather than sit on a shelf.

Multi-piece sets

Matching groups printed together, like a bouquet with its vase.

Custom work

Most of what I print is not in the shop at all. If you have an idea, a photo, or an STL file, send it over and I will tell you whether it is printable and what it will cost. A quote is free — use the Request a Quote form and include rough dimensions, colour, and how many you need.

Common requests: a replacement part that is no longer sold, a scaled model for a presentation, a personalised gift, or a prototype to hold before committing to a manufacturing run.

Bulk and corporate orders

I take larger runs — wedding and event favours, corporate gifts, college project models, promotional pieces. For anything beyond a handful of units, message me with the quantity and your deadline before ordering: batch pricing is different, and I would rather tell you honestly whether a timeline is realistic than take an order I cannot deliver.

How long it takes

Items already listed in the shop: 3–5 days, plus delivery time.

Custom work depends on size, complexity and what is already printing — a small figurine is quick, a large multi-part piece is not. You get a specific timeline with your quote rather than a number that turns out to be wrong.

Delivery and payment

I ship anywhere in India. Shipping is a flat ₹99, and free on orders over ₹2,000. Orders go out with a courier and you get a tracking ID by email when the parcel is dispatched.

Payment is online — card, UPI or net banking, handled by Razorpay. Nothing is charged until you complete the payment.

Why order from someone local

Buying from someone in Pondicherry rather than a national marketplace means you are talking to the person who actually prints the thing. If a piece needs a different colour, a slight resize, or a change halfway through, that is a message rather than a support ticket. And if something arrives damaged, you deal with me, not a returns portal.
