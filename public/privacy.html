Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you use 3d-prints.aswincloud.com, why, and what control you have. We collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • To fulfil an order: your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. We cannot ship without these.
  • If you create an account: your email address, and any name, phone and address you choose to save so checkout can pre-fill them.
  • If you request a quote: what you tell us, plus any reference image you upload.
  • If you use the live chat: your messages, and your name and email if you are signed in.
  • Automatically: aggregate, privacy-preserving visit statistics via Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies or fingerprint you.

What we do NOT collect

We never see or store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Payment details go directly to Razorpay, who are PCI-DSS compliant. We only receive a payment reference and whether the payment succeeded.

Why we hold it

Only to take and deliver your order, to answer you, to send order emails (confirmation and dispatch), and to meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Who else sees it

  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Resend — sending order emails.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, the database, and security.
  • The courier — the name, address and phone needed to deliver.
  • Chatwoot — live chat, self-hosted by us on our own server.

Each receives only what it needs to do its job, and none of them may use your data for their own purposes.

Cookies

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies: one to keep you signed in, and browser storage to remember your cart. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Email aswin@aswincloud.com to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account does not remove order records we must retain by law.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Sessions are signed, admin access is restricted to a verified email allowlist, and payment verification uses a signature check that cannot be forged from the browser.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Anything unclear? Email aswin@aswincloud.com or use the chat on this site — Aswin replies personally.
