If something goes wrong

Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Every item is printed for your order, so this policy is more specific than a general retail one. Read it before you buy — and if something has gone wrong with an order, email aswin@aswincloud.com and it will be put right.

Cancelling an order

  • Before printing starts — email us as soon as possible and we will cancel and refund in full.
  • After printing has started — a ready-made item can usually still be cancelled if it has not shipped. A custom or personalised piece cannot, because it cannot be sold to anyone else.
  • After dispatch — the order cannot be cancelled, but the refund conditions below still apply.

When you can get a refund or replacement

Contact us within 7 days of delivery if:

  • the item arrived damaged or broken;
  • you received the wrong item;
  • the item is significantly different from its description or photograph;
  • the order never arrived.

For damage or a wrong item, send photographs with your message — it is usually all we need. We will reprint the item or refund you in full, whichever you prefer. You will not pay return shipping where the fault is ours.

What is not refundable

  • Change of mind. Each piece is printed to order, so there is no general right of return.
  • Custom or personalised pieces, once printing has started.
  • Normal print variation — slight differences in colour, layer lines or dimensions of a millimetre or two are inherent to 3D printing, not defects.
  • Damage after delivery, including misuse, or use of a decorative print for a structural or load-bearing purpose.
  • An incorrect delivery address supplied at checkout. We will help, but re-shipping may be chargeable.

How a refund is paid

Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Razorpay. Once approved, we process the refund within 3 working days; your bank or card issuer then typically takes 5–7 working days to credit it. Shipping charges are refunded when the fault was ours, and not when an order is cancelled at your request after dispatch.

Failed and duplicate payments

If money left your account but no order appears, do not pay again — email us with the payment reference. Razorpay usually auto-reverses a failed payment within 5–7 working days, and we will chase it for you.

Anything unclear? Email aswin@aswincloud.com or use the chat on this site — Aswin replies personally.
