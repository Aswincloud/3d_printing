Getting it to you

Shipping and Exchange Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Where we ship

Anywhere in India. We do not currently ship internationally — email aswin@aswincloud.com if you are outside India and we will tell you whether it can be arranged.

Charges

  • Flat ₹99 per order.
  • Free on orders over ₹2,000.

The charge is shown at checkout before you pay. A discount is applied before the free shipping threshold is assessed, so a promo code may take an order below ₹2,000.

Dispatch and delivery time

Items are printed after your order is confirmed, so dispatch is not immediate. A typical ready-made item is printed and dispatched within 2–5 working days; larger pieces, multi-part sets and custom work take longer and we will tell you the expected timeline. Once dispatched, couriers usually deliver within 3–7 working days depending on your location.

These are estimates, not guarantees. If a piece is going to take noticeably longer we will contact you rather than leave you waiting.

Tracking

You get an email when your order is dispatched, including the courier and tracking number where one is available. Your order reference looks like AP-xxxxxxxx — quote it in any message about your order.

Failed delivery

If a parcel is returned to us because nobody was available or the address was incomplete, we will contact you. Re-shipping may be chargeable.

Exchanges

Because each item is printed for your order, we do not offer general exchanges — we cannot swap a piece for a different design or colour after printing.

We will replace an item free of charge if it arrives damaged, is the wrong item, or is significantly different from its description. Tell us within 7 days of delivery with photographs. See the Cancellation and Refund Policy for the full terms.

Damaged in transit

Prints are packed carefully, but if a parcel arrives visibly damaged please photograph it before opening where you can. It makes the courier claim straightforward and gets your replacement moving faster.

Anything unclear? Email aswin@aswincloud.com or use the chat on this site — Aswin replies personally.
